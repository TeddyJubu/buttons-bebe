## Policy — measurements

Customer wants item measurements.

Measurements usually require human or warehouse help unless already saved.

## Agent action

Check if measurements are saved.
If saved, provide them.
If not saved, mark Needs staff input at normal priority with the exact item and
size to measure. A missing measurement alone does not require an owner alert.
Do not promise immediate timing.

## Customer response

If the item or size is unknown, ask for that one missing detail. Do not ask again
when the customer has already supplied it. If both are known but measurements
are unavailable, hold the draft for staff to add the measurement; do not send a
generic uncertainty acknowledgment or promise that measurements will be sent.
