## Policy — sizing advice for multiple items

Customer wants sizing advice for multiple items.

Sizing advice should not be guessed. The agent may answer only if reliable fit information exists.

## Agent action

Check product page.
Check product notes.
Check saved previous answers.
Check brand/vendor sizing notes.
If reliable info exists, answer.
If not, use normal staff review.

## Customer response if info is not immediately available

Ask for product links or exact brand/styles only if missing. With identified products and unavailable sizing evidence, mark Needs staff input at normal priority. List each product and the fit/measurement facts staff must verify in missing_facts and staff_next_step. Do not promise a check or follow-up.
